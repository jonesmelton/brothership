# the universe

a scattered civilization of the descendants of humans and the descendants of their creations.

ftl has never been achieved. there is no ansible.

the laser link between worlds has been severed for longer than any world's living memory. signals are never received, automated ships traveling the direct path never arrive.

the only remaining connection between worlds is the 108 Liveship Couriers and their volunteer crews. there will never be more.

to crew a liveship is to go up the mountain: the soul who signs on leaves their world and everyone in it for good, and those left behind tell of them as gallants, the bold ones who went to the rivers and lakes.
