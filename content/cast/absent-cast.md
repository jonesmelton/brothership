# absent cast

The 99 ships in `pennants.tsv` that are not in the starting cast. The starting cast is EF-134 The Sickly General, EF-147 The Tigress, EF-287 The Flea on the Drum, HS-212 The Twin-Tailed Scorpion, HS-226 The Blue-Faced Beast, HS-257 The Prodigal, HS-307 The White Streak in the Waves, HS-325 The Two-Headed Snake, and HS-338 The Turbulent River Dragon.

Ships are in `pennants.tsv` order. Each heading gives the pennant and epithet, then the source figure's romanised name and Chinese conventional name in parentheses. Each description is one sentence, in the third person, describing the figure's character translated into a ship. This file is a basis for blurbs or references if an absent ship needs one; it is not a cast entry, and it does not put a ship in service.

Two ships share the romanised name Zhang Qing: HS-148 張清 The Featherless Arrow and EF-251 張青 The Vegetable Gardener. The Chinese name in the heading tells them apart.

## Heavenly Spirits

### HS-142 The Timely Rain (Song Jiang, 宋江)

The Timely Rain is a generous, well-connected ship that answers distress before it is formally asked, gathers scattered crews and quarrelling brothers into one company, and keeps steering that company toward pardon and a settled peace even when some aboard would rather stay outlaws forever.

### HS-174 The Jade Unicorn (Lu Junyi, 盧俊義)

The Jade Unicorn is a rich, imposing ship of immaculate bearing and formidable reach that was drawn into the brotherhood by scheming and betrayal at home, and it now carries itself as the company's second pillar, reluctant, proud, and hard to move once committed.

### HS-341 The Wizard (Wu Yong, 吳用)

The Wizard is a quiet scholar-ship whose plans arrive several moves ahead of everyone else's, fond of forged signals, borrowed seals, and indirect routes, and happiest when a locked port opens because it believed a story the Wizard wrote for it.

### HS-162 The Dragon in Clouds (Gongsun Sheng, 公孫勝)

The Dragon in Clouds is a remote, weather-wise ship that comes and goes on its own counsel, reads signs in cloud and current that other ships miss, and can turn a crossing aside at the one moment when turning saves the whole company.

### HS-197 The Big Halberd (Guan Sheng, 關勝)

### HS-191 The Panther Head (Lin Chong, 林沖)

### HS-266 The Boat Flame (Zhang Heng, 張橫)

### HS-141 The Recklessly Rash (Ruan Xiaowu, 阮小五)

### HS-177 The Devil Incarnate (Ruan Xiaoqi, 阮小七)

### HS-357 The Pallid (Yang Xiong, 楊雄)

### HS-137 The Rash (Shi Xiu, 石秀)
