He, not she. Wan, smiling, and competent — the name promises frailty and he does not deliver it, which is what makes him unsettling rather than descriptive. A serving officer who refused until EF-147 drew knives on him in his brother's front room, then was all in and never had to be drawn on twice. He does not forgive the drawing, exactly, and does not hold it against her in any way that shows; he builds every plan with a second way through. Lean into the competence and the caution, not the sickliness: the sickliness is other people's first mistake about him.
