## The Sickly General

Earthly Fiend 孫立

### personality and characteristics

a military commander at Dengzhou, elder brother of Sun Xin and a tall, formidable fighter with a sallow, yellowish face that made him a likeness of the sickly Yuchi Gong, skilled with spear and bamboo-jointed steel whip and far from frail in battle; as a serving officer he at first refused to endanger his post for his condemned cousins Xie Zhen and Xie Bao, framed by Squire Mao after hunting a tiger, but when Le He brought word from the prison and Gu Dasao left him no safe refusal—since the rescue would implicate him through kinship in any case—he committed himself wholly, helped break open the Dengzhou prison, took part in the killing of Squire Mao's household, and fled with Sun Xin, Gu Dasao, Xie Zhen, Xie Bao, Zou Yuan, and Zou Run toward Liangshan, where his worth was proved at Zhu Family Village: claiming fellowship as a former fellow pupil of its instructor Luan Tingyu, he entered with his party as supposed Dengzhou reinforcements, won the manor's trust, and acted from within so Liangshan could finally take the village. Seated thirty-ninth on the mountain as one of its cavalry commanders, he followed the band through the amnesty and the campaigns against the Liao, Tian Hu, Wang Qing, and Fang La, survived them all, and in the end returned to Dengzhou with Sun Xin and Gu Dasao, resuming service there as a military officer.
