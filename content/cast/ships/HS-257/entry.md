## The Prodigal

Heavenly Spirit 燕青

### personality and characteristics

a household servant whose parents died young and who was raised in Lu Junyi's house in Daming, Yan Qing is handsome and fair, his body covered with floral tattoos, and, though a servant, surpasses his master in the arts that make his nickname: he plays instruments and sings, speaks the dialects and songs of every region, is a superb wrestler and a crack shot with the crossbow, and moves easily in pleasure quarters as in a great house. When Lu Junyi is lured away by Wu Yong's plot and then betrayed by his wife and his steward Li Gu, Yan Qing is cast out and reduced to begging, yet he follows his exiled master, shoots dead the bribed escorts sent to murder him on the road and, when Lu Junyi is retaken while Yan Qing is away begging food, carries the plea to Liangshan that brings Song Jiang's rescue from the execution ground at Daming. He then takes the mountain with his master, serves among the infantry leaders while remaining Lu Junyi's close attendant, throws the unbeaten champion Ren Yuan in the wrestling ring at Tai'an by timing and skill instead of brute strength, and in Dongjing so wins the trust of the courtesan Li Shishi with his music and manners that she makes him her sworn brother and undertakes to put Liangshan's case for amnesty before Emperor Huizong. He follows the brotherhood through the amnesty and the campaigns against the Liao, Tian Hu, Wang Qing and Fang La, and on the return urges Lu Junyi, his duty done, to retire before success brings ruin; when Lu Junyi refuses, Yan Qing leaves him a farewell letter in the night, takes his bundle and vanishes before any office or reward can be given him.

### relationships

- service: HS-174 The Jade Unicorn (the master in whose Daming house he was raised, whose murder on the exile road he prevented and whom he served until he left him after the Fang La campaign)
- debt: HS-142 The Timely Rain (the Liangshan chief who rescued his master from execution at Daming after he came to the mountain to beg for help)
- sworn: Li Shishi (the Dongjing courtesan who took him as her sworn brother and promised to speak for Liangshan's amnesty to Emperor Huizong)
