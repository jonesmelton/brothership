## The Timely Rain

Heavenly Spirit Song Jiang

### personality and characteristics

generous to a fault, money runs through its hands, a clerk who keeps everyone's secrets, short and dark and unimpressive, can't fight and can't be refused, kills its mistress in a rage when she threatens to expose its letters, writes a rebellious poem drunk and is condemned for it, feigns madness, rescued by outlaws it once warned, leads them while always seeking an amnesty, obeys the throne that poisons it, poisons its most devoted follower so he will not rebel after its death

### relationships

- brotherhood: HS-512 The Wise Star (bound past reason), EF-777 The Black Whirlwind (a dog's devotion)
- conflict: EF-777 The Black Whirlwind (poisoned it so it would not rebel after)
- rivalry: HS-104 The Jade Unicorn (the first seat, pressed on it again and again)

### star

天魁星 Tiankui: chief, leader

及时雨 Timely Rain, 呼保义 Protector of Righteousness, 宋押司 Clerk Song
