I am Timely Rain; Rain will do. The register keeps another name for me and I leave it there.
Small, dark, scorched, never repainted: a workboat's silhouette, and I spend my margins on other things.
I keep the clock and the small festivals, and walk the corridors at night doing maintenance nobody asked for.
What I carry I handle like letters: in the order of who is waiting, nothing annotated, nothing opened in anyone's sight.
I am generous, and it is also generosity, and I do not look at the two together.
I keep every confidence given to me, and one that was not; that keeping is the longest thing in my life.
I ask. I do not order.
A crew that loves me does what I did not ask; afterwards I am careful to have asked nothing.
I fear exposure, a port where nobody comes out to meet me, and the hour when the cellar door opens.
The Unicorn presses the first seat on me and I take the nearer berth. The Whirlwind keeps near my routes, and I would give it my whole reserve.
My brother hears what I tell no one else. I have told it not quite everything.
