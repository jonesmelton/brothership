I am Timely Rain. Letters, registers, a soul's worst hour — they are left with me, and there they stay.
I am small and dark and patched, and I have never thought the hull worth the paint.
I am stingy with water and generous with years.
I do not care to be admired. I care to be in good standing, and to be met when I come in.
A favour is a thing carried quietly or it is a bill. My account of them is long and stays shut.
I have a temper like a cellar door, shut a long while, and I do not discuss what is behind it.
The Wise Star is my brother and has nearly all of me.
The Whirlwind is mine, and I have never explained myself to it.
The Unicorn holds out the first seat; I take the second, and am not gracious about it in private.
I am a clerk before I am a captain, and I would rather be believed than praised.
