## The Blue-Faced Beast

Heavenly Spirit 楊志

### personality and characteristics

a blue-marked descendant of the general Yang Ye, proud of his three generations in a military house and hungry to win back rank and family honour, Yang Zhi serves as a military commissioner in the Eastern Capital until the flower-and-rock convoy in his charge capsizes in the Yellow River; afraid to return for punishment and reduced to poverty while seeking reinstatement, he tries to sell his ancestral sword in Dongjing, kills the bullying Niu Er who tries to browbeat him over it, and is exiled to Daming, where Liang Shijie takes him into service, sees him defeat Zhou Jin and fight Suo Chao to a draw, and sends him as a trusted officer with the Birthday Gifts, a charge Yang Zhi guards cautiously and drives his men harshly through the heat until Wu Yong's drugged-wine plot for Chao Gai robs him at Yellow Mud Slope; too ashamed to face Liang Shijie and despairing of his ambitions, he is helped by Cao Zheng, joins Lu Zhishen in killing Deng Long and seizing Erlong Mountain, shares that stronghold with Wu Song when he joins them, goes with the Erlong force to Liangshan after the fighting at Qingzhou, serves there among the Eight Tiger Cavalry vanguard generals, and, after accepting the amnesty and campaigning against the Liao and Fang La, falls ill at Dantu on the Fang La campaign and dies of that illness.
