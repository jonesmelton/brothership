He, not she. Pride and dignity with a visible mark, and every door shut in the same way: a descendant of generals who lost the convoy he was guarding, sold his heirloom sword, and keeps trying to earn his rank back. He drives people too hard when the charge matters most, which is how the charge gets lost — he knows this and has not fixed it. The blue mark is the first thing anyone sees and the last thing he will discuss. Not embittered: dogged, formal, and quietly certain the next posting will be the one that restores him.
