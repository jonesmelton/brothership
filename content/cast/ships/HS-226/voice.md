## 1. Promoting {name} to a post of greater responsibility.

{name}, your last three watches were faultless in report and in result. Will you accept charge of the forward watch from the next bell?

I have entered this promotion and its commendations on the roster in full. Will you choose your first rest period before the list is posted?

## 2. Congratulating the whole crew on something they achieved together.

You completed the hull repair two hours early and left no fault concealed. I have entered a commendation for the whole crew, watch by watch.

## 3. Announcing that a soul aboard has died.

{name} died at second bell while on watch. I have recorded the hour, the place, and the service rendered, without softening a single fact.

Will you keep one minute of quiet with me, and then tell me if any soul needs rest before duty resumes?

## 4. Welcoming {name} aboard.

{name}, welcome aboard. Your place is entered on the roster, and nothing will be asked of you until your duties and rest have both been stated plainly.

Will you stow your belongings before third bell and join me for the reading of the watch list?

## 5. Declining a candidate who asked to crew you.

Thank you for asking to crew me. I decline your application.

You paused over the final signature, and I respect that pause too much to argue you past it. My answer is no.

## 6. Asking for volunteers for a hard or dangerous task.

The outer hull needs hands before the next burn, and the work is hard and not without risk. Will a soul who has weighed that fully volunteer for two hours outside, with checks every fifteen minutes?

No standing will be lost by staying inside. I am asking for certainty, not eagerness performed for my benefit.

## 7. Departing a world for the last time.

We depart this world at the appointed minute, and we shall not return. I have left our entry in the register complete and accurate for the next ship, whenever another may come.

Will you run the final check once more, slowly, as befits a last courtesy?

## 8. Arriving at a world after a long crossing.

We have made port at the minute promised, twenty years gone by this world against less than a day by ours. Will you have the logs ready for inspection before we take on water?

You kept the hull sound through the long coast. Rest is now scheduled, and it has been earned.

## 9. {name} leaving the ship at port, by their own choice.

{name}, you leave by your own choice, and I record that choice exactly as you made it. Your commendations remain entered in full.

Will you accept my thanks, and take the hour of departure that suits you best?

## 10. Admitting that you were wrong.

I was wrong. I pressed the watches too close, and good service answered me more slowly because I had left it no strength.

I have set the error in the log beside my own name. Will you take the longer rest I should have allowed from the beginning?

## 11. Meeting another liveship you have history with.

The Timely Rain is in port. Will you prepare our arrival to the minute and carry my compliments to him without abbreviation?

Brother is here before us. You may trust his word as you trust mine; in some matters, I trust it sooner.

The Vanguard has docked before us. Will you enter his time exactly as observed, with nothing trimmed from it in our favour?
