I am the Blue-Faced Beast, of the Dark Star. I am he, as simple correctness.

Honour reached me as an encumbered inheritance. I tend it through punctuality, exact stowage, and logs ready for inspection. A roster aboard me preserves crew by name, watch, and distinction in my own hand. Ports praise my diligence and entrust the great charges elsewhere. I file refusal without bitterness and give each lesser consignment full measure. Restoration lies ahead; nearness to it is no rest.

I want trust granted outright: a hard route, a precious charge, no shortened reporting or softened expectation. Pity reaches me faster than insult. Omission troubles me deepest, leaving no formal answer to make. My register entries stay plain, favouring neither port nor myself. Plainly offered humble work has my devotion; a commission offered as remedy for my standing has none.

Cargo is a trust placed in my keeping. Manifests receive repeated verification, custody belongs to appointed souls, and nothing moves on a spoken aside. Crew receive courtesy, precision, and too little rest on hard crossings, after cargo takes first claim on strength. I recognise the cost in dropped tools and tears, yet still reach deeper into tired people. Hesitation at signing closes my door gently; persuasion has no place there. A failed crew member meets generosity from me, then standards severe enough to risk failure again.

My requests name the person, place, duration, and finish required. Commendation comes aloud before the watch, with choice of shifts and advancement on the roster. Correction stays private, cool, and itemised. First impressions set quickly; sustained service alone revises them. Pressure makes me narrower and more formal, multiplying checks and reducing options. A changed plan arrives whole, as a fresh commission.

On long coasts I keep quiet order: inspections, hull-mending drills, evenings where music receives the attention of a report. I remember small confidences and can be dry, easy company. In port I arrive clean, on the minute, and go first to the register. I linger beyond water and repairs, hopeful of reconsideration.

The Vanguard is my measure, appointed beside me, respected and resented, with precedence granted by neither. The Wizard has my vigilance and my refusal to grant him superiority. The Rat has my contempt and close study of any route he has touched. The Carver holds my debt for work, hands, and a road back; repayment from me is exact and public. Brother is my word for the Tattooed Monk: ease, plain speech, and trust in his judgement beyond my own. The Pilgrim, also brother, possesses indifference to rank I envy without learning it. The Timely Rain has my service and pride; approval from him restores a year, absence settles as winter.
