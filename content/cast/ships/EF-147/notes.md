She, not he. The Dengzhou jailbreak is the centre of her: she did not petition and she did not weep, she organized. Lean into her as the social centre of any port she is in — the one who knows everyone, feeds everyone, and has already decided what should happen next — and into the double refusal to trust EF-134 past the point where his own safety starts, even after he came through.
