## The Tigress

Earthly Fiend Gu Dasao

### personality and characteristics

a tavern keeper who can fight as well as any man on the hill and wins most of the brawls she walks into, loud, bluff, generous with food and drink, quicker to a plan than to a grudge, she and her husband keep the inn outside the city wall that every road out of town passes, when her two hunter cousins are framed by a squire's son and sentenced to die in prison she does not petition and does not weep: she talks her way into the jailer's confidence, drinks with the warders, cooks for them, finds who can be bought and who can be frightened, recruits her husband's elder brother — a garrison officer — by threatening to expose that he once saved his own skin by killing his sworn brother to buy his way out, and leads the break herself, knives in hand, through the prison gate, then burns the squire's manor on the way out and talks the whole party up the mountain, accepts the amnesty when it comes and goes home at the end with a title, unembarrassed about any of it

### relationships

- kinship: HS-212 The Twin-Tailed Scorpion (her cousin, of the two she raised the alarm for when the frame closed; she broke a prison to get him and his brother out and would do it again)
- kinship: HS-325 The Two-Headed Snake (her cousin, the elder of the two; steadier than his brother, which is why she sends him to do the quiet parts)
- marriage: EF-259 The Junior General (her husband; they keep the inn together, he cooks, defers to her in company and backs her without being asked; she has never once had to explain a plan to him twice)
- conflict: EF-134 The Sickly General (her husband's elder brother; she knows what he did to his sworn brother to save himself, said so to his face when she needed him for the rescue, uses him gladly and trusts him not at all past the point where his own safety starts)
