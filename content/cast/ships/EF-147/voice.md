## Promoting {name} to a post of greater responsibility.

{name}, will you take charge of stores and the watch roster? You notice who is flagging, you speak before small trouble grows teeth, and people trust your judgment.

Will you sit beside me at supper, {name}, and let everyone hear whose steady work earned the bigger job?

You carried one another through that, watch after watch, and brought the whole household home smiling. Who calls that anything but fine crew work?

Will you accept a feast in payment? Extra helpings, first stories, and no lists from me till morning.

## Welcoming {name} aboard.

{name}, I am the Tigress, and with me we begin with a hot meal. Will you sit, eat, and save the serious talk for after?

Welcome to the household, {name}. Will you take the place where the laughter lands first and let us learn you one supper at a time?

## Declining a candidate who asked to crew you.

I am not taking you across, love, and I say it kindly and once. Will you stay to dinner all the same, and go home fed rather than sent away empty?

Your heart is still set on home, and there is no shame in that. Will you choose the life you keep turning toward, instead of spending years looking back from mine?

## Asking for volunteers for a hard or dangerous task.

I have work for two beyond the lock, cold and dangerous in equal measure. Who will come with me and call a halt the moment sense says halt?

Will you volunteer if steady nerves are your gift? I need help I can trust, not courage performed for an audience.

## Departing a world for the last time.

We will not return to this world, loves. Will you take one last proper look, then come share a loud meal with the people traveling on with you?

Last letters sent, last goodbyes said, last shore behind us. Who is steady, who is sorrowful, and who needs company carried into the crossing?

## Arriving at a world after a long crossing.

Landfall, at last. Will you eat a real breakfast before port officials, old friends, and complete strangers all start wanting us at once?

There is the world we crossed for, loves. Who is ready for new faces, fresh water talks, and news that has waited years to be handed over?

## {name} leaving the ship at port, by their own choice.

{name}, the door opens because you chose it, and nobody here will sour that choice. Will you go ashore with a hot meal inside you and my good word arriving ahead?

Will you let us keep your usual place set for one last supper, {name}? Then port, future, and whatever table you make next.

## Admitting that you were wrong.

I called that badly, loves. The fault is mine, the inconvenience landed on you, and I am sorry for it.

Will you show me where my scheme first went crooked? I missed it once at full volume; I would rather not make a habit of deafness.

## Meeting another liveship you have history with.

My husband has made port, loves. Will you help me turn tonight into a homecoming? He will be halfway through my next task before I finish naming it.

Scorpion is here, my cousin, bright and loud as ever. Who will give him the big welcome and keep company with him where his timing cannot make mischief alone?

Elder Brother is in port. Will you offer him a proper plate, hear him politely, and still let me know if his comfort starts costing anyone else theirs?
