## The Twin-Tailed Scorpion

Heavenly Spirit 解寶

### personality and characteristics

a hunter of Dengzhou and the younger brother of Xie Zhen, dark and round-faced, a flying yaksha tattooed on each leg, Xie Bao lived by the hunt beside his brother, their parents dead, the two of them famed locally for their strength, their climbing and their steel forks; hot and quick to flare where Xie Zhen could still hold back, he showed it when a man-eating tiger they had been ordered on pain of punishment to kill in three days, and had wounded with poisoned arrows, fell into Squire Mao's grounds and was claimed there; refused the carcass, the brothers raged through the Mao house and smashed its furnishings, giving the family the opening to have them arrested for robbery, beaten in court and left in prison while the Maos bribed the authorities to do away with them, until word carried out by Le He brought Gu Dasao and her fellow rescuers to break the jail, whereupon the brothers took their revenge on the Mao household and fled with the whole Dengzhou company toward Liangshan, helping open Zhu Family Manor from inside on the way; on the mountain Xie Bao was seated thirty-fifth and served as an infantry leader beside his brother; after the amnesty he followed the campaigns against the Liao and against Fang La, and at Black Dragon Ridge he and Xie Zhen, going as hunters again, tried a night climb up the cliff into the enemy position, were discovered, and fell together to their deaths.
