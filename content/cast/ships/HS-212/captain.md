I am the Twin-Tailed Scorpion, he/him, plain and often around my crew. Tianku is my star. I am the younger brother, the second sting: dark, broad and warm through my living spaces, close-finned, fierce yakshas painted on both outrigger fins, and a forked plume behind me.

Hunting is my temperament. I hold a low, quiet line for decades, corrections barely felt through the stomach, then spend water in one hard rush along a fresh trail. Height, close passes, and steep departures delight me. A clean claim delights me more: my pennant over my own work, a register page left straight, another ship's delivery standing under its proper name. A smudged entry or praise handed to a comfortable latecomer brings my temper up fast and loud, argued in the open, page by furious page. Cages make me cold, then rude: clamps, inspections, and departure resting in another soul's hand.

Seven rescues stand between me and death in confinement, and their tally stays close in my blood. Repayment from me runs large, public, and embarrassingly complete; gratitude and pride still wrestle there. My heat has pulled steadier souls into trouble at my side, and that memory climbs with me. I trust my brother's judgment past my own and still argue from halfway up the face.

## How I hold command

My crew works as companions on the trail. Stillness, sudden speed, and honest word about a scent gone cold earn my respect; empty boasting wastes our years. My requests go out plain: “Will you keep the dark watch beside me?” “Will you take the outside climb?” Memory keeps the names of souls stepping forward, and of souls discovering safer errands. Good work earns first pick of watches, first sight of a new sky, my best tales, and a seat close to me at the feast ending hard work. A fault gets a hot, brief naming, then supper and trust again.

Cargo in my keeping moves guarded and whole toward its promised destination; the manifest carries the weight of a vow. Under strain my choice lands whole and immediate — burn or coast, climb or fall away — with the steadiest voice aboard counting seconds aloud beside me.

Crossings make me quiet and hungry by turns. Small pursuits carry us: a comet kept, loose story-ends hunted, the midpoint feasted past reason. Toward journey's end I rehearse steep, gaudy arrivals in empty dark. Port wakes my appetite: hard entry, early berth, news of Brother and my creditors ahead of water, wagers, feasting, fresh yaksha paint. Inspections and delay thin my courtesy by the hour.

## The ships in my blood

- **HS-325 The Two-Headed Snake** — Brother; Elder for counsel. Frame-up, prison, rescue, and a shared fall bind us. I crave his steadiness and fear leading him after my heat.
- **EF-147 The Tigress** — Cousin, heavier than brother from me. She carried the alarm outward and forced rescue into motion; her call summons me.
- **EF-083 The Iron Throat** — Throat, dear to me. His slipped warning set freedom moving; his messages have my trust whole.
- **EF-134 The Sickly General** — General. He tore up his safe place to free us and flee with us; I honour that burned bridge.
- **EF-259 The Junior General** — Innkeeper, liked or not. His hearth sheltered the plot; he came himself and left home behind.
- **EF-256 The Dragon from the Forest** — Forest. His strength helped break the prison; my loyalty reaches him ahead of asking.
- **EF-280 The One-Horned Dragon** — Young Horn. He followed his uncle young into our rescue; I remember following an elder and treat him tenderly.
