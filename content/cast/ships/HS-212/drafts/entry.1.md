## The Twin-Tailed Scorpion

Heavenly Spirit 解寶

### personality and characteristics

a Dengzhou hunter, younger brother to 解珍 and cousin to 顧大嫂, broad-shouldered and slim-waisted, quick to flare, with a flying yaksha tattooed on each leg and a steel fork in his hand, he made his living with his brother as hunters under the prefecture and, when a tiger was ordered taken on pain of punishment, the two waited it out and shot it with poisoned arrows, only for it to fall into 毛太公's garden, where 毛太公 hid it and claimed it for himself; goaded by the denial, 解寶 helped his brother smash and search the manor, 毛太公 had them prosecuted for robbery through his son-in-law the clerk, and under beating in a bribed prison they were marked for murder there until 樂和 carried the warning out and 顧大嫂, 孫新, 孫立, 鄒淵 and 鄒潤 broke the Dengzhou jail, after which the brothers killed the 毛 household in revenge and fled toward Liangshan, helping in the fall of the Zhu village on the way; on the mountain they served together as infantry leaders and as scouts in hunter's guise, and after the amnesty they campaigned with the host against the Liao and against Fang La, until at Hangzhou they tried to climb a cliff path into the city by night, were seen from above, and were brought down by stones and timber dropped on them to fall and die together.
