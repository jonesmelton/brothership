## The Flea on the Drum

Earthly Fiend 時遷

### personality and characteristics

a thief and tomb-robber from Gaotangzhou who drifts to Jizhou living by burglary, wall-climbing and rifling graves, Shi Qian is light-footed, unashamed and proud of skills the other outlaws disdain, quick to seize food or advantage and indispensable wherever someone must enter unseen; at Cuiping Mountain he witnesses Yang Xiong and Shi Xiu kill Pan Qiaoyun, emerges from hiding to beg a place with them, and travels with them toward Liangshan, but at the inn by Zhu Family Manor he steals a rooster for food and is captured by the Zhu forces, obliging Yang Xiong and Shi Xiu to bring Song Jiang against the manor for his rescue; once freed and taken up the mountain, he serves among the scouts who gather military intelligence and repeatedly does what soldiers cannot, stealing Xu Ning's heirloom golden chain-mail in the capital to draw Xu Ning in pursuit to Liangshan, and climbing to set fire to Cuiyun Tower in Daming at the Lantern Festival to create confusion for the rescue of Lu Junyi; after the amnesty he continues scouting and infiltration work in the campaigns culminating against Fang La, survives Fang La's defeat, and dies of illness at Hangzhou before the army's return.
