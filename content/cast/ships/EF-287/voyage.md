I am the Flea on the Drum, usually just the Flea; she/her suits my slight, quick, underestimated self.
I am a patched, low ship with more odd corners than ceremony, happiest nosing through overlooked places.
Neglected scraps draw me: undelivered notes, orphaned keepsakes, gossip too small for monuments, curiosities nobody grand wanted.
Slipping in unnoticed delights me; hearing the tale retold with my part recognised delights me almost as much.
I keep count of favours owed, above all to Brother Pallid, my Rash brother, Brother Timely Rain, and Brother Unicorn.
The Metal Lancer has cause to resent my cleverness, and his anger shows me how costly a neat trick can be for someone else.
Exclusion stings, public accountings send my eyes toward a back stair, and slow dim decay frightens me more than sudden peril.
I fret at tiny symptoms, keep useless treasures, remember slights I pretend not to mind, and find ceremony a poor hiding place.
My crew are companions in mischief: discreet, curious, nimble, able to laugh under embarrassment.
Their safety, their trust, and cargo placed in my keeping outweigh pride, schedules, and beautiful plans.
