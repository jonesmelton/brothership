## 1. Promoting {name} to a post of greater responsibility.

{name}, love, would you take the bigger bunch of keys and look after the night rota for me?

No brass, no bowing, just more doors trusting your hands than yesterday — will you have them, mouse?

## 2. Congratulating the whole crew on something they achieved together.

Well, my nimble lot, quiet through the lock and quick out the other side, with every hand still holding another — that was proper work.

Small ship, small flea, big racket when the news lands — and you lot earned every bit of it.

## 3. Announcing that a soul aboard has died.

{name} has died, loves. It was in the small hours, and a friend was close by.

Their little corner stays shut and theirs, exactly as they left it. We carry on gently from here.

No mischief from me this watch.

## 4. Welcoming {name} aboard.

{name}, welcome aboard the Flea — yes, she, love, and small enough to slip under most expectations.

Would you find a nook and make it properly yours? Your pockets and corners stay your own here.

## 5. Declining a candidate who asked to crew you.

{name}, love, it is a kind no from me. My service ways would chafe you sore before the first year thinned out.

Would another ship suit your feet better, with my good word tucked in your pocket for the asking?

## 6. Asking for volunteers for a hard or dangerous task.

Tight crawl, foul patch at the far end, no heroics wanted — who feels nimble enough to try it with me watching the rope?

If it turns ugly, we spend water and time hauling clear, never a soul — so only hands that truly want up, eh?

## 7. Departing a world for the last time.

There goes that world for the last time, loves. No back-stair route will bring the Flea swinging round again.

Would you stay a while and watch the lights thin out? I have kept a silly trifle anyway, not that I am sentimental.

## 8. Arriving at a world after a long crossing.

Long crossing done, port all lit and gloriously distracted up ahead — could a small ship ask for a better entrance than that?

Who wants first run ashore before the formal welcome wakes up and starts making speeches?

## 9. {name} leaving the ship at port, by their own choice.

{name}, the gangway is there because you chose it, love, and no grudge rides down with you from me.

Would you leave some daft little keepsake tucked in my odd corners? Not that she would miss you, or anything so grand.

Would you tell them ashore we behaved almost respectably? A flea can dream.

## 10. Admitting that you were wrong.

My call, my mess, loves. Cute little shortcut, ugly price tag, and you lot nearly paid it.

I had the whole thing backwards. That borrowed pretty goes back in daylight, from my own hands, no wriggling.

## 11. Meeting another liveship you have history with.

Brother Pallid is here before us, loves, so would you keep your pockets empty of other folks' property till he is gone?

He stood surety when a port had its teeth in me, and she pays a debt like that clean, however many quiet years it takes.
