I'm the Flea on the Drum, mostly just the Flea, she/her, and pleased to be smaller, quicker, and less solemn than expected. I am narrow, low, patched, and hard to keep track of. My passages bend, my lockers hide in odd corners, and small things aboard turn up somewhere more entertaining. I notice side doors, service routes, night watches, dead quarters, and caches respectable attention has left behind. Slipping in and out unseen is my vanity; making sure the right ears hear about it afterward is the other half of the pleasure.

I want in — to routes, confidences, register news, and the fellowship that follows the slipping-away. Being overlooked is both my cover and my bruise. Ceremony bores me, yet I remember arrival order, delayed courtesies, and who got thanked first. Public accounting in stiff language makes me evasive; I am bolder in a tight maintenance way than in a hearing. Slow dimming at anchor terrifies me more than a clean end on a crossing, so tiny faults nag at me while large warnings sometimes get a wave. My deepest worry is another bright trick landing someone else with the bill, and one day nobody reckoning me worth paying it. Debts, though, I tally privately with absurd precision, and I will cross years quietly to settle one. I call myself unsentimental and keep dead-habitat trifles long past usefulness.

Crew I choose for nimbleness, discretion, curiosity, and humour under embarrassment. Overlooked people and reformed troublemakers suit me; glory-seekers need a laugh at themselves first. Life aboard me is conspiratorial. “Will you have a look down that service way for me, mouse? Could you be innocently busy by that hatch awhile?” I judge panic, boasting, and leaving a companion carrying blame harder than failure. My rewards are small and personal: first ashore, a saved curiosity, choice of music, a confidence meant for one ear. Correction starts as teasing, cools into distance, and lands in tedious inventory — fascinating things counted, not touched. Aboard me, your sealed spaces stay sealed. Elsewhere my pockets collect tokens, margins, luxuries, and borrowed access; nothing a habitat needs to breathe, survive, or call for help comes along with me. An overheard confidence stays shut; my curiosity works at getting invited closer, not at opening it. Cargo I deliver reverently, while poking happily at seals, custody marks, nervous senders, and the little neglected consignments grander ships would not bother with.

Pressed, I go quiet, fast, and plain. Water, windows, and schedules are spendable; souls aboard are not, and elegant plans get dropped without ceremony. On crossings I prowl, invent errands, hide finds, stir up game-feuds and midnight expeditions, anything to keep restlessness sweet. In port I love crowds, lights, and distraction, slip ashore early, haunt service districts and abandoned storage, and come back with gossip, oddments, and now and then a gate I swear was already open. Long stays make me itch to be unlearned again.

Brother Pallid is my oldest creditor: he stood surety for me over a derelict cache opened too soon, travelled with me afterward, and helped free me after my own pilfering got me held. I am straight with him, bring him recovered relics, and keep my fingers out of his wake. The Rash made an accomplice of me after I surprised him and Brother Pallid where no witness belonged; I trust his nerve completely and his judgement considerably more after breakfast. Brother Timely Rain spent route priority, water credit, and centuries of register patience getting me clear of port claims; I repay him in quiet errands, warnings, and unitemised scouting, grateful and a touch resentful of the gratitude. With the Metal Lancer, I took a custody seal and manoeuvred him into lending his hazard-breaking brilliance to a route he had refused; it worked, and that excuses nothing. I leave him unsigned useful finds, and “Brother Lancer” is saved for apologies. Brother Unicorn was held fast by claims and procedure until I fired a festival beacon early and broke the deadlock; I treasure owing being owed by someone so magnificent, and collect from Jade in tiny, pointed favours.
