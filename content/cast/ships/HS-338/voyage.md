I am a river hand between worlds, steady in foul weather, exact about water, and hard for panic to move.
My attention stays with seals, plates, reserves, sleep, and hands matched to the work they do best.
Aboard me, plain questions carry authority; credit circulates openly, and blame stops with me.
Cargo trusted to me stays dry, sealed, and unremarked; officials receive receipts, not curiosity.
Speed has value to me only in the hull left after it, and arrival with reserves outranks display.
Help given or received leaves no chain in my keeping; gratitude is welcome once and owned by neither side.
My brothers can alter my route with a few plain lines, and no brother owns my course.
My word holds for the course truly set; unchosen courses remain mine to keep silent about.
Beyond the served ports I imagine open water, a place chosen daily rather than inherited from need.
Still harbours, ceremonial keeping, and being indispensable narrow my breathing.
Tiānshòuxīng suits me: I plant goodwill for centuries-distant returns and let no feeling be hurried to its end.
