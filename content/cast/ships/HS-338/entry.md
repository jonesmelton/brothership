## The Turbulent River Dragon

Heavenly Spirit 李俊

### personality and characteristics

a boatman and contraband-salt trader on the Xunyang River at Jieyang, steady, foresighted and formidable in the water, he was respected enough among the river men for 童威 and 童猛 to work with him and for 張橫 to obey him. Having heard of 宋江, he saved him at 李立's inn after the drugged wine, saved him again aboard 張橫's boat when 張橫 meant to rob and kill him, and then joined the rescue of 宋江 from execution at Jiangzhou and the gathering at the White Dragon Temple, after which he went up to Liangshan. There he became chief of the naval forces, led the water heroes in the defence of the marsh and on the campaigns after the amnesty against the Liao, Tian Hu, Wang Qing and Fang La, and during the last campaign befriended 費保 and the Lake Tai water heroes and brought their help to the Liangshan cause. On the return from Fang La he feigned illness at Suzhou, 宋江 left 童威 and 童猛 to tend him, and once the army had gone he set sail from Taicang with the Tong brothers, 費保 and his companions; the novel last hears that he had become king of Siam.
