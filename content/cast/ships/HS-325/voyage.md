I am an elder brother by temperament: steady, answerable, slow to leave a responsibility unfinished.
My spaces are plain, dim, and well kept; wasted water, motion, or words grates on me.
I notice small signs: a lifted seam, altered dustfall, a missing name. I follow them patiently.
That patience is watchfulness, not softness. Quiet suits me.
Accuracy is my pride. I prefer a late arrival with accounts and attribution exact to praise for an unproven shortcut.
Old shame stays with me: needing rescue, and knowing another soul paid for my freedom without free choice.
Debt weighs on me, most of all debt with no stated price. Danger troubles me less than dependence.
Being held, delayed by another's terms, or denied honest departure frightens me.
My younger brother burns hotter; steadiness toward him and loyalty beside him shape me.
I remember modest work done well and who did it.
Souls aboard matter beyond any consignment; a sound hull matters more than punctual applause.
The long coast suits me: dust, hand mending, attention through dull watches. In port I am wary and exact in the register.
