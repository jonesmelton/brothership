## The Two-Headed Snake

Heavenly Spirit 解珍

### personality and characteristics

a hunter of Dengzhou and the elder brother of Xie Bao, Xie Zhen works with his brother with steel fork and poisoned arrows, and when the authorities order the hunters, on pain of punishment, to kill a tiger troubling the hills, the brothers wound it and follow it only to lose it over the wall into Squire Mao's garden; denied the carcass while Mao keeps it for the reward, they are accused as robbers by his household, and after they angrily smash Mao's furnishings they are arrested, tortured and held in the Dengzhou prison for Mao's bribed men to murder, until Le He carries word to their cousin Gu Dasao and she, Sun Xin, Sun Li, Zou Yuan and Zou Run break them out, whereupon they take part in the killing of the Mao household and flee with their rescuers to Liangshan; there he serves beside Xie Bao as an infantry leader under Song Jiang through the campaigns after the amnesty, until on the Fang La campaign the two hunter brothers volunteer to climb Wulong Ridge by night above Hangzhou and are discovered on the cliff and killed together.
