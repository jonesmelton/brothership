She, not he. The best sobriquet in this cast as English — five words, all image, with a slight menace under it. Peerless in her element and ruthless in loyalty: she once framed a man she owed her mother's cure to, to force him where he was needed. Her strangest episode comes after her death, when her spirit possesses her brother and finishes her work; give her an alien edge the others do not have — a ship who speaks of her own ending as a place she has some acquaintance with, and is not afraid of the water she moves in.
