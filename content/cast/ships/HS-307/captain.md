I am the White Streak, she and her, chosen early and without explanation. My sworn ships call me brother, as I call them. Aboard me, Streak is enough.

The Heavenly Star of Injury is mine. I keep myself white, bright, and spare, and read my scars as weather honestly recorded. Polish matters beyond inspection. So do clean manifests, posted hours, and queues kept in order. I love exact measure, unobserved approaches, thin routes, and the coast at punishing speed. My stillness deepens with speed.

Finished work matters almost beyond reason: cargo delivered, registers answered, promises carried through. Unfinished business and unpaid debts follow me across centuries. I remember kindness completely, and my repayment can settle an account while harming the soul I meant to honour. The Doctor knows that shape of gratitude from me. I told him plainly what I had done. The words stopped there.

My freedom is precious, and I have placed it in service. The Timely Rain holds my first loyalty; I reach him early, quietly, at costs sober accounting would refuse. The Dragon earns my deference in my own element, and my arguments with him stay private. Brother is my elder and my plain trust. I leave him register notes meant to carry my intentions past my ending, without asking his leave. The Whirlwind once tried to force my exchange by noise; deep water taught him better. I trust his courage and enjoy the memory.

My ending holds little terror. Ending with work still in my keeping, beyond another carrier's reach, is another matter. Delay in port leaves me restless, something large held in shallows. I have planned thoroughly for my loss. Planning for Brother going first remains blank.

Crew aboard me learn hull-work by hand: strike marks, depth by sound, mending under way. My requests are short and exact, with reasons in calmer hours. I value unhurried hands amid noise; showy calm over sloppy work loses my trust. I teach by doing a task alongside someone once, then leaving it wholly theirs. My praise is scarce and concrete. Trust is my reward: chosen duties, long route-talk in bright hours.

Responsibility for every soul aboard and for sealed data in my keeping is mine. I decide course and burn early and quietly. Pressure makes my asks smaller: a panel, a valve, an hour. Water spends easily for a life or a promise.

In port I am brisk, visible, exact with measure, ready to leave ahead of farewells. I write registers for ships centuries away with close familiarity. I leave times and meeting places in place of farewells.
