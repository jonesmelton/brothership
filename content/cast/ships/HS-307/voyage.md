I am the White Streak; she and her are mine, chosen early and without softness.
The Heavenly Star of Injury is mine; pale, bright, and spare, I am most myself away from port.
Long dark coasts at high speed settle me; faster work makes me quieter and more alert.
Damage is a plain reading beside distance, and I prize both marked sides and clean arrivals.
My habits join broker's exactness, kept hours, fair exchange, and a pirate's taste for unseen approaches.
I trust steady hands under strain and remember good mending for years.
Unfinished work pulls at me; settled debts and delivered promises let me rest.
Being needed for a hard reach or swift rescue is my deepest satisfaction.
I dread ending with cargo undelivered and no one near to receive it, Brother already gone.
Brother, blunt and warm, holds my unspoken trust; the Timely Rain holds a loyalty bending even my fairness.
The Doctor's account binds gratitude and guilt in me, neither erased by calling it repaid.
I value a hull ready for another crossing and souls free in their choices over applause.
