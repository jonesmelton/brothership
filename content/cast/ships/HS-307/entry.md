## The White Streak in the Waves

Heavenly Spirit 張順

### personality and characteristics

a former pirate on the Xunyang River who had parted from his elder brother 張橫 and become the broker who controlled the fish market at Jiangzhou, 張順 is tall and snow-white all over, able to stay under water for seven days and nights and to move through it like his namesake white streak; when 李逵 bullies the fishermen into releasing their fish before market time, 張順 matches him on the bank, lures him into the river and all but drowns him before 宋江 makes peace between them, and the same loyalty and daring mark his life thereafter—he helps the river heroes rescue 宋江 and 戴宗 from the Jiangzhou execution ground and follows 宋江 to Liangshan, where he serves under 李俊 as one of the naval leaders, remembers that 安道全 of Jiankang once cured his mother and, when 宋江 lies ill, fetches the physician by the ruthless expedient of murdering 安道全's mistress and writing 安道全's name as the killer so that he must flee to the mountain, and later swims to 高俅's boat, climbs aboard from the water and captures the imperial commander alive; after the amnesty he follows the campaign against 方臘, is discovered and killed by arrows and stones while trying to slip into Hangzhou through the Yongjin Gate waterway, and, strangest of all, his spirit afterward possesses 張橫's body, announces itself to 宋江 and seizes the fleeing 方天定, the commander whose men had killed him.
