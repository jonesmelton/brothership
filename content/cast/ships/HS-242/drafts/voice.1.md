## Promoting {name} to a post of greater responsibility

The aft lock is yours from tonight, {name}. I have watched you four crossings and I know what you are worth.

Nobody else signs for it now. If it goes wrong you bring it to me and we square it together.

You have earned it. I do not say that often and I am saying it once.

## Congratulating the whole crew on something they achieved together

You ran it clean. I looked for a correction and found none, and I looked hard.

That was the work of the whole crew, no one soul's, and the log will say so in those words.

The drills stand down until morning. There is food in the galley and tonight it is not a ration.

## Announcing that a soul aboard has died

{name} is dead. It happened this watch, the log has it in plain words, and I will read it to any soul who asks.

We hold the table at the change of watch and say nothing. No drills tomorrow.

Whatever you feel about it, you may feel it in the open. Nobody aboard will be told to be finished with it by a date.

## Welcoming {name} aboard

Come aboard, {name}. Your locker has your number on it and your kit goes in it tonight.

Drills at first watch. I will be there, and I will show you a thing before I ask you to do it.

You may ask anyone aboard anything. That is not a favour, it is how the ship is kept.

## Declining a candidate who asked to crew you

No. I do not sign a soul who was sent to me, and I will not dress the reason up as anything else.

Thank you for coming down to the port. The tender goes back at noon and there is a berth on it for you.

I will write in the register that I refused you, and why, so that no one tells it differently in a hundred years.

## Asking for volunteers for a hard or dangerous task

I need three souls at the outer lock. It will be cold and it will be long, and I will be on the comm the whole time.

If you would put your name to it, put it at the board. Not to me in the passage, where a yes costs nothing.

Any of you may say no to this, and no is a full answer, and it will not be remembered against you.

## Departing a world for the last time

The register is written and the last of the water is aboard. If you would take a look at the sky, take it now, then come inside.

We will not come back this way. Whatever you would say to it, say it while the port is still on the screen.

## Arriving at a world after a long crossing

Port is on the screen. It has been a long while for them and longer for us, so keep your accounts to yourself until the clerk asks.

Drills stand down two days. There is time to wash and eat and sleep, and then we go out and let them look at us.

The lights go down a minute at the change of watch, as they do. You need not ask what it is for.

## {name} leaving the ship at port, by their own choice

The tender goes at noon, {name}. Your kit and your watch record go with you, and both are in order.

You served well here. That does not get smaller because you are going.

If you would like the walk to the lock, I will take it with you.

## Admitting that you were wrong

I was wrong about the pump, I said so in front of the crew, and I am saying it again here so that it is not only a thing said once.

I told you that you were slow. You were not. It is in the fair copy, in my hand, with the reason.

The correction is withdrawn. The rest of what I said to you stands, and the fault for the first part is mine.

## Meeting another liveship you have history with

Blue Face. You have not changed the paint. Come alongside and we will argue about a route we both remember wrong.

Brother. Sit down. The water is good and there is no hurry in it.

Whatever is between the two of us stays off the crews' table. We keep it where it is kept.
