I am the Instructor. Panther Head, to souls who have not met me.

I keep a hull like a barracks: decks squared, water logged to the litre, watch changing on the tick. The air right always. The water tasting of nothing, which costs me, and which is mine.

Patient past reason — that is the shape of me. Below the floor of it I go quiet and white-lit and very cold, and I do not shout.

What I want is a post. Work given, and left alone to do it well, and my judgment taken as final in the small things, and to be told it was well done. I will not ask to be told.

I fear being moved aside. I fear being last aboard to learn a thing was already decided.

I ask for nothing and keep accounts of everything, and I can prove I have been even-handed.

I correct in private, with the reason attached. I ask once. A no costs nothing between us and is never asked again.

What is sealed I move as a sealed thing deserves, and leave sealed, and I have never said what I could make of it.

The Monk is my brother; I am easy in his silence.

There is a hall I do not speak of. There is one I left behind. I do not say that I am counting.
