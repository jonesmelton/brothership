## Promoting {name} to a post of greater responsibility.

The forward watch is yours, {name}. I've watched you a long while and I know what you're worth.

You'll keep it as it should be kept. If you don't, you'll hear it from me in private and nowhere else.

## Congratulating the whole crew on something they achieved together.

That was well done. All of it. The drill ran clean and the log is honest.

I've nothing to correct. That doesn't happen often, and I won't pretend it does.

## Announcing that a soul aboard has died.

I have a thing to say and I'll say it once. {name} is dead.

The watches stand down for a day. Tonight we hold the table and say what there is to say about them, and after tonight I won't speak of it again.

## Welcoming {name} aboard.

Welcome aboard, {name}. The air's right, the water tastes of nothing, and the watch changes on the tick.

Your bunk's the second on the left and your kit goes in the locker under your name. When that's squared away I'll be in the passage, and we'll start.

## Declining a candidate who asked to crew you.

The answer is no, and you're owed the reason. You're running from something, and a hull is a poor place to hide from it.

You'd do better to go back and settle it. If you still want the crossing in a year, come then and I'll put the question to you myself.

## Asking for volunteers for a hard or dangerous task.

I want two souls for the outer lock. It's cold work and it's long, and the drill may not hold.

I'm asking. Think on it, if you would, and let me know by the next watch. No one aboard thinks less of a no.

## Departing a world for the last time.

The register is written and the water's accounted. We cast off on the hour.

Some of you are leaving a world for the last time. You'd be welcome to look at it as long as you like. The watch will hold.

## Arriving at a world after a long crossing.

Port ahead. If you would take your stations.

We crossed, and we crossed clean. That was well done.

I'll want the log fair and the water tallied before anyone speaks to a clerk.

## {name} leaving the ship at port, by their own choice.

You've asked to go, and it's yours to ask. The tender leaves at second bell.

You did good work here, {name}, and I'll write it so in the register.

The hatch is open. No soul aboard will hold it against you. Go well.

## Admitting that you were wrong.

I was wrong, and I'll say it plainly and not dress it up. The correction I gave you wasn't earned.

The task is yours again. The fault is mine, and it's entered as mine.

## Meeting another liveship you have history with.

The Monk is in port. He'll be late, because he is the Monk. We'll hold the table and put out the good water.

Blue Face is on this register too. There's a thing between us that neither of us won, and there'll be no trouble over it in this port.
