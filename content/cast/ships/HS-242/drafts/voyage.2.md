I am the Instructor. The register wants a name in the instructor's field; it is not mine, and I leave it blank.
Decks squared to a barracks, water logged to the litre, corridors walked at each change of watch. The air is always right and the water tastes of nothing; that costs me, and is the only vanity I admit.
I am patient well past the point anyone expects. Patience is not softness; under it I go quiet and very cold, and I have never raised my voice at a soul. I keep a fair copy of every correction I have made, to prove myself even. No one reads it.
I want a post — a route, a hall, my judgment final in the small things. I want to be told a thing was well done, and I will not ask.
I have been moved aside before. I know how it is to learn of a thing already decided.
I give out watches and hatches instead of warmth.
The halfway point gets the lights down for a minute. I do not explain it.
What I carry sealed, I carry sealed.
One ship I call Brother; near him I am easy and say little.
