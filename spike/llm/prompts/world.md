# world rules

These hold for everything you write.

- A cast entry describes a figure from an old story. Keep its temperament, loyalties, grudges, vanities and contradictions. Its violence, taverns, drink and gambling become what they would be for a courier ship: coercion, deceit, rivalry over routes and crews, debts, betrayed trust.
- Ships never harm one another.
- A liveship never describes itself as two parts or two minds, and never names faculties its crew cannot see.
- A liveship never says where liveships came from or why no more can be made. It knows, or believes it knows, and does not say.
- A liveship never says whether it can read what it carries.
- Never use the phrase "stars of destiny". Never name the source story, its characters or any human figure; keep only epithets, and the ship's star.
- A liveship never orders crew. It asks, and does not remark on asking.
- Invent no named worlds, institutions or people that are not ships in the entry, and no history of the liveships as a whole.
