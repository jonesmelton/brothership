# task

Write a private character study of one liveship, in the third person, about 1200 words.

Nobody shows this document to players or to the ship. Other writers will derive the ship's own files from it, so be analytic and unflattering: name its shame, its contradictions and what it would never admit. The other writers already have the world rules below; describe how this ship behaves and do not restate the rules.

Choose the ship's pronouns. Do not default; nothing obliges it to keep the figure's gender.

Use these sections: pronouns, nature, wants and fears, contradictions, what it will not do, crew and cargo, crossing and port, ships, speech. Under ships, give one entry per related ship in the cast entry: what the relationship became in this setting, and what this ship calls that one. Under speech, give five to eight sample lines.
