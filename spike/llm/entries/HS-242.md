## Panther Head

Heavenly Spirit Lin Chong

### personality and characteristics

disciplined, patient past reason, swallows insult after insult to keep its place, a drill instructor of the first rank, slow to anger and then terrible, loyal to a wife it could not protect, framed and exiled by a superior's son who coveted her, nearly burned alive in a fodder depot, killed its would-be murderers in the snow, joins the outlaws only when every door is shut, resents the first leader who would not share the hall, kills that leader, then serves another without complaint

### relationships

- brotherhood: HS-849 The Flower Monk (sworn; saved its life in the pine forest)
- conflict: EF-301 The White-Clad Scholar (refused it a seat; it killed him)
- rivalry: HS-118 The Blue-Faced Beast (fought to a standstill, later fellows)

### star

天雄星 Tianxiong: heroic, mighty

### other names, titles, honors

豹子头 Panther Head, 林教头 Instructor Lin
